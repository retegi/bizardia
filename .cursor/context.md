# Project Context

This project is a Django web platform for managing a cultural and gastronomic association.

The system allows members to manage activities, reservations, and internal services.

Main features:

- member (partner) management
- dining room reservations
- point of sale (TPV)
- product management
- blog and news publishing
- photo galleries
- association history pages

The system is used by association members and administrators.

## Main Concepts

Partner
Represents a member of the association.

Product
Represents items that can be sold in the TPV system.

Dining Room Reservation
Members can reserve tables in the dining room.

TPV
Internal point of sale used for selling products.

Activity
Events organized by the association.

Blog / News
Content published for members and visitors.
